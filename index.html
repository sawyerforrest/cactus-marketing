Why the rates are better

Your volume,
plus everyone else’s.

Carriers price on volume — the more parcels sit behind an agreement, the better the rate behind it. On your own, you are quoted on what you ship. Through Cactus, your parcels are priced off the whole pool.

Illustrative — Cactus pools client volume under its carrier programs.

Let’s talk
Questions

Before you switch.

Do I have to change my WMS or shipping software?
No. Cactus provisions carrier rate access on the system you already run — your WMS, TMS or OMS. Your team ships exactly the way they do today. There is no Cactus software for them to learn and no workflow to rebuild.
How long does onboarding take?
Typically one to two weeks from agreement to shipping on Cactus rates. Most of that is carrier-side provisioning rather than anything you need to do.
What actually happens when a carrier reweighs a package?
The carrier bills the higher weight weeks later, usually buried in a file with thousands of other lines. We compare that line against your contracted rate, flag the difference, and hold it for your review instead of passing it through to your invoice.
Who files the claim when a package is lost or damaged?
We do. Carrier claims management is handled by Cactus end to end — filing, follow-up and resolution. A client-facing view of claim status is in development.
How does billing work?
Clean weekly invoices, one per carrier, reconciled line by line so each one ties back to the carrier invoice it came from. Carrier charges and billed amounts sit side by side.
Do you sell USPS rates?
No. Cactus does not hold or sell USPS rates. USPS is available by referral to an unaffiliated partner if you need that lane.
Can I see what I’m spending?
Every invoice shows carrier charges and billed amounts side by side, and cost trend analysis is available on request. A self-serve client portal with claims, invoices and spend is in development.
What does a comparative rate analysis involve?
Send us a parcel export from your platform. We rate your actual shipments against Cactus carrier cards and show you the delta, line by line, before you commit to anything.
